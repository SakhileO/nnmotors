About N&N Motors

A family-run dealership that's been part of Bloemfontein's motoring community for over a decade.

Our story

Over 10 Years of Trusted Service

N&N Motors has been selling quality pre-owned vehicles to Bloemfontein motorists for more than a decade. What started as a small dealership on Oliver Tambo Street has grown into a name locals recognise for straightforward dealing and cars that are properly looked over before they're sold.

We welcome trade-ins and arrange finance through leading South African banks, and every deal we put together follows the FICA, FAIS, NCS and CPA regulations that govern responsible motor trading — so you can buy with confidence, not just hope.

Above all, we're a small team who'd rather earn a customer for the next ten years than rush one sale. If a car isn't right for you, we'll say so.
