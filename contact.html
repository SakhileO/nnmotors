Frequently asked

Common Questions

Do you offer finance on your vehicles?
Yes — we arrange finance through ABSA, WesBank, Standard Bank and Nedbank MFC, and can usually get pre-approval within 24 hours. Use the calculator on our Finance section to get an idea of monthly repayments.
Can I trade in my current car?
Absolutely. Bring your vehicle in for a no-obligation valuation, or start the process online via our Sell Your Car page. Most trade-ins are valued the same day.
Do you assist customers outside Bloemfontein?
Yes, we help buyers from across South Africa — we can arrange delivery or transport for out-of-town customers, just call us to discuss the details.
Are your vehicles inspected before sale?
Every vehicle on our floor is checked over before it's listed, so you can buy with confidence.
What are your opening hours?
We're open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays.

Prefer to just browse first?

Have a look through our current stock, or let us know what you're after and we'll help you find it.
